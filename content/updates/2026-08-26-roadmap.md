[Roadmap](/roadmap) is live. It has four sections, and which one a piece of work
sits in is the only commitment attached to it.

### The section is the status

Exploring means we are still deciding, and nothing in it is agreed. Planned
means it is agreed and queued, in no particular order. Building means someone is
on it now. Shipped points at the write-up here on updates, so there is one copy
of the detail rather than two.

There are no dates anywhere on the page. A date we cannot keep is worse than no
date, and the section already tells you the thing a date would be standing in
for.

### Every entry ends in a question

Each open entry has one thing we want to know from you, and it is the last thing
on the entry page. On the CI steps entry it is which of your pipeline steps we
would get wrong. On sign-in it is anything about your
organisation's setup that would stop us reading repos as you.

Answers go to #devx for now. Voting and comments on the page itself need to know
who you are, so they are waiting on sign-in, which is itself on the roadmap.

### Entries are files in this repo

An entry is a markdown file in `content/roadmap`, so adding one is a pull
request and the wording gets reviewed like anything else. Nothing is copied out
of our tickets, which means the page says what we would say out loud rather than
what a ticket happened to be called.

If you want something on there, a pull request works and so does #devx. Either
way it shows up in Exploring first.

### Building is empty on purpose

Nothing is in flight this week, and the page says so rather than borrowing
something from Planned to look busy. What we pick up next comes out of
Exploring, which is the part you can change.
